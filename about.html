About Filtbo

Filtbo is a free Bible app made by Gospel Animation. Our goal is simple: to help people read, hear and understand the Word of God every day, wherever they are.

What you can do with Filtbo

Read the Bible in several translations, search Scripture, save your favourite verses, follow reading plans with reminders, listen to the Audio Bible, and ask Geelko AI questions as you study. Selected features are available to Premium members, and the core Bible reading stays free.

Who we are

Gospel Animation is a Christian creative ministry that makes videos, animations and tools to encourage faith. Filtbo grew out of that same desire to make Scripture easier to reach and easier to love. You can follow our work on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp and Spotify under the name Gospel Animation.

Our promise

We want Filtbo to be a clean, respectful and family-friendly place for Bible study. We welcome your feedback, and we use it to keep improving the app.

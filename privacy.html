1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Filtbo, information may include your name, email address, Google account identifier, optional profile photo, language and appearance preferences, saved verses, reading progress, reading plans, Premium status, AI prompts and conversation history, and technical information required for app functionality. If you use AI image or voice features, the relevant image or audio is processed as part of your AI request.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve Filtbo, authenticate accounts, synchronize supported data, restore your preferences, provide AI responses, maintain Chat History, manage Premium access, deliver reading-plan and offline-download notifications when you permit them, prevent abuse, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Guest and Local Data

When you use Filtbo as a guest, supported profile and activity information is primarily stored in your browser or device storage. Offline Bible content is cached locally so supported reading features can work without internet access. Local storage can be lost if you clear app/browser data, uninstall the app, or reset your device.

4. Sign-In and Account Data

If you sign in with Google, Filtbo uses secure services to authenticate you and to store supported account data. This allows supported information, including your profile, reading activity, settings, and AI Chat History, to be associated with your account. Access controls are used to restrict account data to the signed-in user where applicable.

5. AI Chat and AI Processing

When you use Geelko AI, the text you send and information needed to produce a response may be transmitted to a trusted AI provider used by Filtbo. AI processing is used to generate the requested response. Filtbo does not use your AI conversations to advertise to you. The AI provider may process requests according to its own service terms and privacy practices.

AI features may also receive images and voice messages when you choose to use those tools. Geelko AI may analyze submitted images and may use AI image-generation or image-editing services when you request those features. Image features are subject to the usage limits shown in the app. We recommend not sending passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information to AI Chat.

6. Chat History and Retention

If Chat History is available and enabled, signed-in conversations are saved under your authenticated Filtbo account so you can reopen them from AI Settings → Chat History. You can manually delete individual conversations. Filtbo's current retention design is up to 3 months for non-deleted conversations, followed by removal through the app's retention process.

7. Images, Voice and User Content

Images, recordings, messages, and other content you voluntarily submit are used to provide the feature you requested. Filtbo may process an image so the AI can understand or describe it, and may transmit image or voice requests to the configured AI provider for processing. When image generation or editing is requested, the relevant prompt and, where applicable, source image may be processed by the AI image service used by Filtbo.

Image features are subject to the usage limits shown in the app. Limits are intended to control service usage and may be enforced using account and technical information. You control whether you submit such content. Please avoid submitting another person's private information without appropriate permission.

8. Offline Data and Notifications

Offline Bible downloads are stored on your device for supported offline use. If you enable notifications, Filtbo may store the information needed to deliver your reading-plan reminders and offline-download completion notification, such as a notification subscription endpoint and reminder details. Notifications can be disabled through your device or browser settings. Delivery depends on the notification platform and your device settings.

9. Premium Payments

If you purchase Premium, payment details are handled by the secure payment provider presented during checkout. Filtbo is designed not to receive or store your full card number, expiry date, or CVV. We may receive or store limited transaction information needed to recognize Premium status, such as subscription status, account association, and expiry information.

If you choose to make a donation, you are taken to the payment provider's secure page. Filtbo is designed not to receive or store your card details. We may receive limited information needed to acknowledge the donation, such as the amount, date and the name or email you choose to provide. Geelko AI settings you choose, such as answer length or style, are stored on your device and are sent with your AI message only so the answer can follow your preferences.

10. Advertising

Filtbo may display advertising, through trusted advertising partners, where applicable. Advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to measure and serve ads according to their own policies and your available settings. Premium users may receive an ad-free experience where Filtbo states that Premium includes it.

11. Third-Party Services

Depending on the features you use, Filtbo may interact with trusted providers for sign-in, AI, payments, Bible content, notifications, advertising, and other technical needs. Each provider may process information under its own privacy policy and terms. We aim to send only information reasonably needed for the feature being used.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures available to Filtbo, including authenticated access controls for supported account data. No internet transmission, device, browser, or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should keep your device and account protected and avoid sharing login credentials.

13. Children's Privacy

Filtbo is family-friendly but is not intentionally designed to independently collect personal information from children under 13. Where local law sets a higher age, that higher age applies. Parents or guardians should supervise younger users and contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

14. Your Choices and Rights

You may use Filtbo settings to change supported profile information, appearance and language preferences, manage notifications, delete AI conversations, sign out, or delete your account where those controls are provided. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights under applicable law, including rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information.

15. Data Retention and Deletion

Local data generally remains on your device until you remove it, clear app/browser data, or uninstall Filtbo. Account data is retained according to the feature's purpose and your account status. AI Chat History is designed for up to 3 months unless you delete it sooner. When you delete your account, Filtbo will remove supported account data from its systems, subject to limited technical, legal, fraud-prevention, or payment-record requirements where retention is necessary.

16. International Processing

Third-party providers used by Filtbo may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. By using the relevant features, you acknowledge that processing may occur internationally, subject to applicable law and the providers' policies.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Filtbo gains new features, changes its services or providers, or when applicable law changes. Material updates will be reflected in the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically, especially before using new features such as AI, notifications, or Premium services.

18. Contact Us

Privacy questions, deletion requests, or other concerns can be sent to filtbooffcial@gmail.com.

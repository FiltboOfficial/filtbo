Galatians

Paul writes strongly to churches that were being told they needed to keep the Jewish law to be saved. He insists that people are made right with God by faith in Christ alone, and that Christians live in freedom led by the Spirit. The book is often called the charter of Christian freedom.

Key themes in Galatians

  • Justification by faith
  • Freedom in Christ
  • The fruit of the Spirit

Key passages to read

  • Galatians 2:20: I have been crucified with Christ
  • Galatians 3:26–29: all one in Christ
  • Galatians 5:16–26: the fruit of the Spirit

A reading tip

It is short enough to read in one sitting. Try reading it aloud.

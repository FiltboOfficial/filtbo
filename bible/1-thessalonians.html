New Testament · Paul's Letters

1 Thessalonians

Paul encourages a young church facing persecution. He thanks God for their faith, love and hope, urges them to live to please God, and comforts them about believers who have died and the return of Christ.

Chapters5
AuthorPaul
WrittenAbout AD 50–51
SectionPaul's Letters

Key themes in 1 Thessalonians

  • Faith, hope and love
  • Holy living
  • The return of Christ

Key passages to read

  • 1 Thessalonians 1: a model church
  • 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18: the hope of the resurrection
  • 1 Thessalonians 5:16–18: rejoice always, pray continually

A reading tip

Each chapter ends with a reference to Christ's return. Watch for it.

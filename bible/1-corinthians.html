1 Corinthians

Paul writes to a gifted but divided church in Corinth. He answers questions about unity, morality, marriage, worship, spiritual gifts and the resurrection. The book includes the famous chapter on love and the clearest account of the resurrection of the dead.

Key themes in 1 Corinthians

  • Unity in the church
  • Love as the greatest gift
  • The resurrection of Christ

Key passages to read

  • 1 Corinthians 12: one body, many parts
  • 1 Corinthians 13: the way of love
  • 1 Corinthians 15: the resurrection

A reading tip

Remember that each section answers a real problem. Ask what problem Paul is addressing before reading his answer.

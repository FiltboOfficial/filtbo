Nahum

Nahum announces the fall of Nineveh, the capital of Assyria, about a century after Jonah's preaching. The book shows that God is both slow to anger and just, and that cruel empires will not stand forever. It brings comfort to those who suffered under oppression.

Chapters3
AuthorNahum
WrittenAbout 663–612 BC
SectionMinor Prophets

Key themes in Nahum

  • God's justice
  • The end of cruel power
  • Comfort for the oppressed

Key passages to read

  • Nahum 1:2–8: the Lord is a stronghold
  • Nahum 2: the fall of Nineveh
  • Nahum 3: the final judgment

A reading tip

Read Jonah first. Nahum shows what happened when Nineveh later turned from God.

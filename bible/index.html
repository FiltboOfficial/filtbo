Bible Library: all 66 books of the Bible

Browse every book of the Bible with a plain-language summary, the main themes, key passages to read and a simple reading tip. Whether you are starting the Bible for the first time or preparing a study, this library helps you understand where each book fits in God's story.

The Bible is a collection of 66 books written over about 1,500 years. The Old Testament has 39 books and tells how God created the world, called Israel and promised a Saviour. The New Testament has 27 books and tells how Jesus Christ came, how the church began and how God will make all things new.

When you are ready to read, open the free Filtbo app. You can read in several translations, listen, search, save verses and follow reading plans.

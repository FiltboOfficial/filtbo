Ezekiel

Ezekiel was a priest and prophet who spoke to the exiles in Babylon. Through visions and dramatic actions he explains why Jerusalem fell, then promises a new heart, a new spirit and a restored people. The book closes with a vision of God's presence returning to his temple.

Key themes in Ezekiel

  • The glory of God
  • Personal responsibility and renewal
  • Restoration and a new heart

Key passages to read

  • Ezekiel 1: the vision of God's glory
  • Ezekiel 34: God the true shepherd
  • Ezekiel 37: the valley of dry bones

A reading tip

Read the strange visions as pictures with a message. Each one points to God's holiness or his promise to restore.

Job

Job is the story of a righteous man who loses his family, wealth and health. His friends insist that he must have sinned, but Job holds on to his integrity and cries out for answers. When God finally speaks, he does not explain the suffering, but reveals his wisdom and power. Job learns to trust a God he cannot fully understand.

Key themes in Job

  • Suffering and faith
  • The limits of human wisdom
  • God's greatness and sovereignty

Key passages to read

  • Job 1–2: the testing of Job
  • Job 19:25: I know that my Redeemer lives
  • Job 38–42: God answers Job

A reading tip

The friends' speeches contain true statements used wrongly. Read them carefully rather than as God's final word.
